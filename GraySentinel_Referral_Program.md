### **PROGRAM OVERVIEW**
**"Refer 1 Friend = 1 Month Free"**

#### **How It Works:**
1. **Existing Client** refers a friend
2. **Friend** signs up for any plan
3. **Both** get 1 month free service
4. **Referrer** gets additional rewards

#### **Referral Rewards:**
- **1st Referral:** 1 month free service
- **5th Referral:** 2 months free service
- **10th Referral:** 3 months free service
- **20th Referral:** 6 months free service
- **50th Referral:** 1 year free service

---

### **REFERRAL PROGRAM TERMS & CONDITIONS**

#### **ELIGIBILITY:**
- Must be an active GraySentinel subscriber
- Referred friend must be a new customer
- Referral must complete payment and onboarding
- Both parties must maintain active subscriptions

#### **REWARDS:**
- Rewards are applied to next billing cycle
- Rewards cannot be converted to cash
- Rewards expire after 12 months
- Maximum 1 year free service per customer

#### **PROCESS:**
1. **Get Referral Link:** Share your unique referral code
2. **Friend Signs Up:** Uses your referral code during signup
3. **Verification:** We verify the referral
4. **Rewards:** Both parties get free service

#### **REFERRAL TRACKING:**
- Unique referral codes for each customer
- Real-time tracking dashboard
- Email notifications for successful referrals
- Monthly referral reports
