## 🎯 PRICING PSYCHOLOGY TACTICS

### **1. Anchor Pricing**
Always show "value" before "price":
- Total Value: ₹2,499
- Your Price: ₹1,999
- Savings: ₹500

### **2. Price Framing**
- Not: "₹999 per month"
- Yes: "Just ₹33 per day"
- Or: "Less than your monthly Netflix subscription"

### **3. Good-Better-Best**
Always show 3 tiers:
- Basic (anchor low)
- Premium (most choose) ⭐
- Fortress (anchor high, makes Premium seem reasonable)

### **4. Limited Time Offers**
- "First month 50% off - This week only"
- "Only 3 slots left for free workshop"
- Creates urgency without being pushy

### **5. Social Proof Pricing**
- "100+ families trust Premium Shield"
- "Most chosen by businesses like yours"
- Reduces decision anxiety

### **6. Bundling**
- Buy annual = 3 months free (not "pay for 9")
- Frame as bonus, not discount
- "₹10,188 for 13 months of protection"

## 🎓 PRICING BEST PRACTICES

### **DO:**
✅ Test different price points
✅ Survey customers on willingness to pay
✅ Grandfather existing customers when increasing prices
✅ Offer annual discounts
✅ Bundle services for perceived value
✅ Show ROI clearly (cost of scam vs cost of prevention)

### **DON'T:**
❌ Compete on price alone (race to bottom)
❌ Change prices too frequently (confuses customers)
❌ Hide fees (damages trust)
❌ Offer discounts without reason (devalues service)
❌ Forget to increase prices annually (inflation, value addition)

**Review pricing every 6 months:**
