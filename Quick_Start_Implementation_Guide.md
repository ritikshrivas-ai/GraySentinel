## 🎯 COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

### **Week 1 Mistakes**
❌ **Perfectionism:** Don't wait for perfect website/content - launch with good enough
❌ **Feature Creep:** Stick to core services initially, don't overcomplicate
❌ **Underpricing:** Don't compete on price alone, focus on value
❌ **Overcommitting:** Don't promise what you can't deliver consistently
❌ **Neglecting Follow-up:** Always follow up within 24 hours

### **Scaling Mistakes**
❌ **Hiring Too Fast:** Ensure revenue before adding team members
❌ **Expanding Too Early:** Dominate Gurugram before expanding geographically
❌ **Ignoring Metrics:** Track everything from day 1
❌ **Poor Customer Service:** Maintain quality as you scale
❌ **Cash Flow Issues:** Monitor cash flow weekly, not monthly
