## 📞 OBJECTION HANDLING CHEAT SHEET

|-----------|-------------------|
| "Too expensive" | Compare to cost of ONE scam (₹20K-50K avg) vs yearly cost (₹12K). Share payment plans. Mention referral program. |
| "I'm tech-savvy" | Acknowledge, then educate on NEW threats. Scammers evolve daily. Share recent sophisticated scam. Position as "research team" doing homework for them. |

| "Is this really necessary?" | Story time: Share recent local scam victim. Statistics: 1 in 3 families targeted. Ask: "Can you afford to be in that 1/3?" |

## 🎯 CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION TIPS

1. **Always offer multiple options** (anchoring effect)
   - Basic vs Premium (most choose Premium)
   - Monthly vs Quarterly vs Yearly (yearly seems better value)

2. **Use scarcity genuinely**
   - "3 slots left this month"
   - "Offer valid until Sunday"
   - But don't fake it - hurts trust

3. **Make saying YES easy**
   - Free trial (no credit card)
   - Money-back guarantee
   - Cancel anytime
   - Pay after 7 days (trial period)

4. **Follow-up sequence**
   - Day 1: Thank you + resources
   - Day 3: Scam alert (value add)
   - Day 5: Quiz (engagement)
   - Day 7: Offer (conversion)
   - Day 14: Final reminder
   - Then weekly newsletter

5. **Track everything**
   - Which script converts best?
   - Which objection is most common?
   - What follow-up timing works?
   - A/B test and optimize
