## 💡 PRICING STRATEGIES

### **Penetration Pricing (Launch Phase)**
- **50% Discount** for first 100 customers
- **Family Referral Bonus:** 1 month free for each successful referral
- **Annual Payment Discount:** 2 months free on annual plans
- **Student/Senior Discount:** 30% off family plans

### **Value-Based Pricing**
- **ROI Calculator:** Show potential savings vs. fraud losses
- **Comparison Charts:** Position against expensive enterprise solutions
- **Success Stories:** Highlight money saved by existing customers
- **Guarantee:** "Prevent one fraud incident or get 6 months free"

### **Psychological Pricing**
- **₹999 vs ₹1,000:** Just under round numbers
- **Daily Cost Breakdown:** "₹33/day for complete family protection"
- **Coffee Comparison:** "Less than your daily Starbucks"
- **Bundle Savings:** "Save ₹2,000 with annual plan"
