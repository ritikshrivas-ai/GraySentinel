### **DAY 9 - TUESDAY**

**LinkedIn (10 AM):**
```
Phishing vs Vishing vs Smishing: Know the difference 🎣

All are social engineering attacks, but different methods:

📧 PHISHING (Email)
→ Fake emails pretending to be banks/companies
→ Links lead to fake websites
→ Example: "Your account will be closed, click here"

📞 VISHING (Voice/Phone)
→ Scam calls claiming to be officials
→ Ask for OTP, card details, passwords
→ Example: "Sir, we're from bank, tell us your OTP"

📱 SMISHING (SMS)
→ Fake text messages with malicious links
→ Usually urgent/threatening tone
→ Example: "KYC expired, update here: [link]"

How to protect yourself:

For Phishing:
✅ Check sender email carefully
✅ Hover over links before clicking
✅ When in doubt, visit official website directly

For Vishing:
✅ Never share OTP/PIN on call
✅ Hang up and call official number
✅ Banks never ask for such details

For Smishing:
✅ Don't click links in SMS
✅ Verify with official source
✅ Delete suspicious messages

Remember: If it creates urgency, it's probably a scam.

Questions? Drop them below 👇

#Cybersecurity #OnlineSafety #GraySentinel #Education
```
