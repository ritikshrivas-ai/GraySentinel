## 🚧 COMMON CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

### **Challenge 1: "I'm posting but no one's engaging"**

**Diagnosis:**
- Content not resonating
- Wrong platform/timing
- Lack of CTA

**Solutions:**
- Use scam alerts (high engagement)
- Post at peak times (7-9 AM, 1-2 PM, 7-8 PM IST)
- Add clear CTA: "DM 'SAFE'", "Comment below", "Tag a friend"
- Engage first (comment on others' posts before posting yours)

---

### **Challenge 2: "Leads are coming but not converting"**

**Diagnosis:**
- Poor qualification
- Pricing mismatch
- Trust issues

**Solutions:**
- Use qualification questions from Sales Scripts
- Offer 7-day free trial (removes risk)
- Share testimonials early in conversation
- Follow up consistently (most sales happen on 5th+ touch)

---

### **Challenge 3: "I don't have time for all this content"**

**Diagnosis:**
- Not batching
- Perfectionism
- No systems

**Solutions:**
- Batch: Create 7 days of content in one 4-hour session
- 80/20: Focus on highest ROI platforms (LinkedIn + Instagram for you)
- Repurpose: 1 LinkedIn post → 3 Instagram stories → 1 Reel → 1 Tweet thread
- Use scheduling tools: Buffer (free tier) or Later
- Done > Perfect: Post consistently, even if imperfect

---

### **Challenge 4: "No one is signing up for paid plans"**

**Diagnosis:**
- Too early (trust not built)
- Wrong audience
- Value not clear

**Solutions:**
- Lead with value: 2 weeks of pure help before pitching
- Free workshops → Build trust → Then sell
- Testimonials: Get 3 testimonials even if you do service for free initially
- Clear ROI: "Prevent ₹50K scam for ₹999/month"
- Guarantee: "Cancel anytime, no questions asked"
