EXAMPLE:

🚨 NEW SCAM ALERT: "Gurugram Cyber Police" Fake Calls 🚨

We've received 12 reports this week of scammers impersonating Gurugram Police.

HERE'S HOW IT WORKS:

Step 1: You get a call from number starting with +91 (looks legit)
Step 2: Caller says "This is Inspector [Name] from Gurugram Cyber Police"
Step 3: "Your Aadhaar is linked to illegal activity / money laundering"
Step 4: "Press 1 to speak to officer, or FIR will be filed automatically"
Step 5: If you press 1, they ask for "verification" - OTP, bank details, etc.
Step 6: Your account emptied

WHY IT WORKS:
→ Fear factor (nobody wants FIR!)
→ Authority figure (police = must comply)
→ Urgency ("immediately or FIR filed")
→ Sophistication (they may know your address)

RED FLAGS - IT'S 100% SCAM IF:
❌ Police calling from random numbers (real police visit in person for serious matters)
❌ Asking for OTP/passwords/bank details (police NEVER do this)
❌ "Press 1 to connect" (automated scam system)
❌ Threats over phone (real legal process = written notice)

REAL POLICE PROCESS:
✅ Written notice sent to your address
✅ You visit cyber cell in person
✅ Verification through official channels
✅ NO phone calls demanding immediate action

HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF:
1️⃣ If you get such call: HANG UP immediately
2️⃣ Don't press any buttons
3️⃣ Don't call back the number
4️⃣ Block the number
5️⃣ If worried, visit Cyber Police Station (Sector 51) in person
6️⃣ Report scam call to: cybercrime.gov.in

ALREADY FELL FOR IT?
⚠️ ACT FAST (within 24 hours):
1. Call your bank immediately: Block cards, freeze account
2. File complaint: cybercrime.gov.in or 1930
3. Visit Cyber Police Station: Sector 51, Gurugram
